🪐 Meet All 8 Planets

☿ 1. Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest to the Sun. It has a rocky surface covered with craters and almost no atmosphere.

Type: Rocky planet

Year: 88 Earth days

✨ Did you know? Mercury has extreme temperature changes between day and night.

♀ 2. Venus

Venus is similar in size to Earth. Its thick atmosphere traps heat, making it the hottest planet in our solar system.

Type: Rocky planet

Atmosphere: Mostly carbon dioxide

✨ Did you know? Venus rotates in the opposite direction to most planets.

🌍 3. Earth

Earth is our home planet. It has liquid water, a protective atmosphere and the conditions needed for life as we know it.

Type: Rocky planet

Surface: About 71% water

✨ Did you know? Earth is the only planet currently known to support life.

♂ 4. Mars

Mars is called the Red Planet because iron-rich dust gives its surface a reddish colour. Scientists study it to understand whether life could have existed there.

Type: Rocky planet

Moons: Phobos and Deimos

✨ Did you know? Mars has Olympus Mons, a gigantic volcano.

♃ 5. Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet. It is a gas giant with colourful cloud bands, powerful storms and many moons.

Type: Gas giant

Famous feature: Great Red Spot

✨ Did you know? The Great Red Spot is a giant storm.

🪐 6. Saturn

Saturn is a gas giant famous for its beautiful rings. These rings contain countless pieces of ice and rocky material.

Type: Gas giant

Famous feature: Ring system

✨ Did you know? Saturn's average density is lower than that of water.

♅ 7. Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant. Methane in its atmosphere gives it a blue-green colour. Its rotation is unusually tilted.

Type: Ice giant

Colour: Blue-green

✨ Did you know? Uranus rotates almost on its side.

♆ 8. Neptune

Neptune is the farthest recognised planet from the Sun. It is a cold ice giant with powerful winds and a deep blue appearance.

Type: Ice giant

Year: About 165 Earth years

✨ Did you know? Neptune takes about 165 Earth years to orbit the Sun.
